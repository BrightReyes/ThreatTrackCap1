Help is always within reach. No delays, no hesitation.

When you're in danger, navigating complex menus is impossible. ThreatTrack features a fail-safe SOS gateway with a 3-second tactile hold meter that prevents accidental triggers while locking your GPS coordinates and dispatching to the nearest police community precinct.

  • check_circle Release before 3 seconds and the hold resets instantly — no false alarms sent
